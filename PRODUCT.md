# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MeetMap is used by Windows desktop users who need to capture meetings, preserve local artifacts, process valid audio after the meeting, and export structured summaries and meeting maps. Users are likely reviewing work meetings, bilingual conversations, and action-heavy discussions where accuracy, retryability, and privacy matter.

## Product Purpose

MeetMap records system audio and microphone input, processes usable tracks through cloud transcription and meeting intelligence, then produces a Word summary and standalone HTML meeting map. Success means a user can move from a recorded meeting to trustworthy local artifacts without wondering what was captured, uploaded, generated, or saved.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, technical, precise. The interface should feel like a reliable desktop work tool: dense enough for repeated use, clear about state, and restrained in visual expression.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a cold enterprise SaaS admin console. Avoid generic corporate dashboards, decorative AI-tool templates, and crowded meeting-software chrome.

## Design Principles

1. Make capture and processing state explicit.
2. Keep sensitive data choices visible and plain-language.
3. Preserve workflow momentum for repeat users.
4. Separate setup, recording, processing, and exports clearly.
5. Favor stable desktop patterns over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Text contrast, keyboard access, visible focus states, form labels, and reduced-motion behavior should be treated as product requirements, not polish.
